# Customer Analytics & Churn Modeling

**Context:** Telecom customer analytics at Hexaware Technologies  
**Focus:** Churn prediction, behavioral features, NLP, and large-scale data processing

## Problem

Customer usage, billing, complaints, and support history provided signals for customer retention and revenue analysis. The work focused on turning these sources into consistent, useful analytics.

## Approach

- Analyzed subscriber and customer data with Python, SQL, Pandas, Spark, and Databricks.
- Engineered behavioral features and applied XGBoost to predict churn and customer behavior.
- Used BERT for complaint classification and PyTorch LSTM models for sequence patterns.
- Improved data processing and validation workflows and delivered reporting through Tableau and Power BI.

## Reported outcomes

- Prediction error decreased by **17%** during validation.
- Analysis surfaced **$680K in potential revenue opportunities**.
- Analytical processing time decreased by **34%**.
- Source-data consistency improved by **27%**.

## Technology

Python, SQL, Pandas, XGBoost, BERT, PyTorch, Spark, Databricks, Tableau, Power BI, AWS.
