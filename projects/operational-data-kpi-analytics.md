# Operational Data & KPI Analytics

**Context:** Backup telemetry and license-renewal analytics at Veritas Software  
**Focus:** Data quality, operational reporting, and statistical analysis

## Problem

Operational backup and license data needed validation and clearer reporting for planning and performance reviews. The analysis covered large telemetry datasets and recurring management KPIs.

## Approach

- Queried backup telemetry in Redshift and cleaned S3-hosted records with Python and Pandas.
- Validated operational datasets with Python, SQL, Pandas, and NumPy.
- Built Tableau and Power BI dashboards for storage utilization and license-renewal KPIs.
- Used exploratory analysis and hypothesis testing to examine backup failures, renewal trends, and performance fluctuations.

## Reported outcomes

- Dataset completeness improved by **26%**.
- Manual reporting effort decreased by **30%**.
- Analysis identified **$310K in potential cost-saving opportunities**.
- Investigated **14% performance fluctuations** to clarify contributing factors.

## Technology

Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Amazon Redshift, Amazon S3, Tableau, Power BI.
